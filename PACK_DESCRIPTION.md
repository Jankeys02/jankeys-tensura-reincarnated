# Jankeys' Tensura Reincarnated

Reincarnate as a slime (or anything else) in a NeoForge 1.21.1 world built around *That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime*. A quest book narrated by the "Voice of the World" teaches you the pack as you play, from your first day to the endgame bosses.

**What's inside**
- **Tensura and its addons:** Ice and Fire, Nightmare, Better Subordinates, Guild, Enigmatic, Unique Monsters, Ascension, TensuraMoreSkills, Elite Tensura Addon and Origins.
- **A 31-chapter quest book (830 quests)** with a short tutorial in every quest: the controls, Tensura, the extra dimensions, boss hunts, homestead life and a chapter for each addon.
- **Ranked Paths:** quests grant ranks as you progress, with perks such as a bigger Ultimine, claimed chunks, homes, bonus health and a Tensura skill per rank.
- **Dimension gate:** in survival, Eternal Starlight, Chrono Dawn and The Afterdark stay locked until you have visited the Nether or summoned a Wither.
- **Difficulty-scaled spawns:** the strongest mobs only spawn naturally on higher difficulties. Spawn eggs and commands still work.
- **World and loot:** Terralith biomes, four extra dimensions (Twilight Forest, Eternal Starlight, Chrono Dawn, The Afterdark), YUNG's structures, Dungeons Arise, Cataclysm, Mowzie's Mobs, Apotheosis, and homestead mods like Farmer's Delight and Supplementaries.
- **Performance and visuals:** Sodium, Iris, ModernFix, FerriteCore and Distant Horizons, plus optional Fresh Animations and shaders.

**Requirements**
- Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249, Java 21
- 6-8 GB RAM allocated (8 GB with shaders or Distant Horizons)
- Recommended Java arguments: `-XX:+UseZGC -XX:+ZGenerational` (avoids Distant Horizons stutter)

**Server pack**
A ready-to-run dedicated-server pack is attached to each release as an additional file (mods, configs and start scripts inside; the readme in the zip has the steps). A few mods can't be bundled because their authors block it; they are listed in `MISSING-MODS.txt` in the zip and set as dependencies on the file. Back up your world before updating: world generation mods only change newly generated chunks.
